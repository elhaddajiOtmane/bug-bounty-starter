# Lab 08 — Your first bug bounty program (no hacking yet)

**Goal:** pick the right program, read it properly, and set up to hunt legally. This lab is all business setup — the part most hunters skip and then fail at.

## Steps
1. Create accounts on HackerOne and Bugcrowd (free). Complete your profile properly — programs check it.
2. Use `bug-bounty-business`: pick **3 candidate public programs** with wildcard scope and note for each: launch/recency, scope size, bounty table, what they exclude.
3. For your #1 pick, do the `recon-scope-triage` exercise on paper: list in-scope, out-of-scope, excluded bug classes, testing rules. Copy the rules into your notes word for word.
4. Create **two test accounts** on the target (two emails). Log in as both and click through the app as a normal user for 20 minutes. Write down the 5 main features.
5. Start your tracker spreadsheet: Date | Program | Asset | Class | Status | Payout. Add today's row: "setup + scope read."

**Done when:** you can say the program's rules from memory, you have two working test accounts, and your tracker exists. Next lab turns a lab finding into money format.
