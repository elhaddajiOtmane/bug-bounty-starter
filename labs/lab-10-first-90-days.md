# Lab 10 — The first 90 days plan (routine that leads to a first payout)

**Goal:** turn skills into a system. Bug bounty pays the consistent, not the occasional.

## The weekly routine (5 sessions, ~2h each)
- **Mon — Recon & learn:** pick the week's surface; read 3 disclosed reports in your class and copy their structure into notes.
- **Tue/Wed — Deep hunt:** `/bounty-hunt` on ONE feature of ONE program. Two-account testing. Depth over breadth.
- **Thu — Hunt + validate:** finish tests, run `triage-validation`, kill weak candidates without mercy.
- **Fri — Report & review:** `/bounty-report` for anything validated. Update the tracker. One hour of blue-team notes: how would each finding be detected/fixed? (This doubles your value — purple skills.)

## Milestones
- **Day 30:** labs 01–07 done, 1 program known deeply, 10+ hunting sessions logged, first reports submitted (duplicates at this stage are tuition, not failure — log what the duplicate taught you).
- **Day 60:** one class specialized (access control and API flaws pay beginners best), disclosed-report notes habit running, signal protected (no speculative submissions).
- **Day 90:** review the tracker like a business: which program, class, and surface produced results? Double down there. Set the next quarter's target: first payout → private program invites → steady monthly number.

## Rules that protect the money
Authorization and platform rules always (`.agents/rules/bug-bounty.md`). No scope, no test. Your reputation (signal) is your real asset — it compounds; one careless month can zero it.
