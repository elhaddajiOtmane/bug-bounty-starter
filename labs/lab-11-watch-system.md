# Lab 11 — Set up your first-mover watch system

**Goal:** build the habit and the lists that put you on fresh surface before the crowd. One-time setup ~1 hour, then 15 minutes a day with `/scope-watch`.

## Setup steps
1. Pick your **3–5 home programs** (from Lab 08's shortlist): you will know these better than the crowd does. Save a copy of each program's scope page in your notes with today's date — this is your diff baseline.
2. Run subdomain enumeration on your #1 program (`hunt-subdomain` steps) and save the host list with the date. This is the second baseline.
3. Bookmark: each platform's new-programs listing, each home program's scope page and product changelog/"what's new" page.
4. Create your **fresh list** (a note or sheet): Surface | Program | Date seen | Hunted? | Result.
5. Do a first full `/scope-watch` pass now and put at least one entry on the fresh list (a new host, a new feature, a new program — whatever you find first).

## Daily driver
Every hunting day starts with `/scope-watch` (15 min), then you hunt from the fresh list. Weekly: re-diff subdomains for one home program, rotating through the list.

**Done when:** baselines are saved, the fresh list exists with its first entry, and tomorrow's session is already decided by the list. Being first is now a routine, not a hope.
